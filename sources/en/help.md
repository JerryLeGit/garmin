# Help

**How do I install a watch face?**
From the **Connect IQ** app on your phone: find the watch face, tap "Install", then pick it as your watch face on the watch. The watch must be connected to the phone.

**How do I change its settings?**
In the Connect IQ (or Garmin Connect) app on your phone: open the watch face installed on your watch, then its settings. Settings are not changed on the watch; they apply as soon as you save.

**A value shows "--".**
The watch has not provided it yet (just after installing, watch off the wrist, no reading yet) or your model does not measure it. It shows up as soon as it is available. Details for each value: [Watch data](data.html).

**Does the watch face drain the battery?**
No. Heavy calculations are done once and kept, and the screen is redrawn only once a minute at rest, like a Garmin watch face.

**Daylight saving time, travel, time zones?**
The watch faces follow the watch's time and recalculate when the time or time zone changes.

**How do I uninstall?**
In the Connect IQ app: open the watch face installed on your watch, then delete it.
