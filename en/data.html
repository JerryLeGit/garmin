Watch data

The watch faces show data read on the watch, as a gauge (bar or ring) or a text field, depending on the watch face and your settings. They are shown for information only; some exist only on some models.

Reading a gauge

  • Theme colour: normal progress.
  • Green: goal reached (the gauge stays full when you go past it).
  • Red: alert (threshold crossed, see each value).
  • Grey track only: value unavailable, or no goal set.
  • A gauge is never completely empty once a value exists: a short mark stays visible, even at zero.
  • Above 1,000, numbers switch to thousands: "8.2k" for 8,240 steps.

The values

Value What is shown Gauge
Steps Steps today. Towards the watch's step goal.
Floors Floors climbed today. Towards the watch's floors goal.
Intensity minutes Total for the week. Towards the watch's weekly goal.
Calories Calories today. (text field only)
Heart rate During an activity: the live value. Otherwise: the last valid reading of the past 10 minutes. From your resting HR to your max HR, split by your zones. Red in zone 5.
Body Battery Level, in %. 0 to 100%. A white mark shows today's highest level. Red at 5% or below.
Stress Score from 0 to 100. In four steps. Red from 75.
Recovery Recovery hours left; "Ok" once you have recovered. Full and green once you have recovered. Adjustable scale (48 h by default). Red beyond the scale.
Sport Your activity totals: distance, duration or number of sessions, over the week, month or year, for one sport or all. Towards the goal you set, in the watch's unit (km or mi, hours, sessions).
Battery Charge in %, or the battery life estimated by the watch (in hours under 48 h, in days above). Red below the threshold you set (20% by default), green while charging.
Notifications Number of pending notifications. (text field only)

Sports available for the Sport gauge: running, cycling, e-bike, walking, hiking, swimming, cross-country skiing, rowing, inline skating, paddleboarding, kayaking, snowshoeing, or all sports.

When are they read?

  • Every minute, and when you raise your wrist: steps, floors, intensity minutes, calories, Body Battery, stress, battery, notifications.
  • Every second while the screen is on after a gesture: heart rate.
  • Every 15 minutes: the recovery gauge (as a text field: every minute).
  • Every hour, when the watch face starts and when the day changes: sport totals.

Good to know

  • Sport: totals come from the watch's activity history, which the watch face keeps over time. On the day you install it, it knows the activities of the past 40 days; the year total then fills up with every new activity.
  • Body Battery: today's highest level is tracked by the watch face. On the day you install it, the morning peak is only known if it is still in the watch's recent history.
  • Stress and Body Battery show the last valid value of the past hour: the watch does not compute them all the time (watch off the wrist, activity in progress).
  • "--": the value is not available yet, or your model does not provide it.
